How these checks work & what they can’t tell you
Public records, explained in context
DMARC-able reads public DNS records in your browser. Results are a snapshot, not a complete email security audit or a guarantee of delivery. A missing record does not prove that anyone has impersonated your domain.
DMARC and SPF
We look for a DMARC TXT record at _dmarc.yourdomain and an SPF TXT record at the domain. We describe the published policy without validating every tag or following the complete SPF lookup tree. Subdomain policy inheritance is not evaluated. Confirm your sending services before changing either record.
DKIM
We try a small set of common DKIM selectors. Your provider may use another selector, so “Not confirmed” does not mean DKIM is missing. Check a recent sent message’s headers or your provider’s settings for the actual selector.
HTTPS and MX
HTTPS is a browser reachability check, not a certificate audit. Browser or network restrictions can prevent it. MX records identify published mail receivers; their presence does not confirm delivery, and their absence does not rule out SMTP fallback.
Privacy and drafts
No API key is required. Checks run only on request. Domains are sent to Cloudflare’s DNS resolver and, for HTTPS, the target website. Drafts are assembled locally from the findings. Recipient names and edited drafts stay in this page and are not saved after a reload.
